parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The parser reads the month first, so any date whose day is twelve or less comes out with day and month swapped: booked for 2 July, invited for 7 February. Only those dates have a swapped twin, and the reports only ever name days of twelve or less. This is the bug #4471 describes. It covers app, email and the booking-v2 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form prints the wrong hint

Three tenants run booking-v3. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY under it. Their customers type what the hint says, month first, and the form then reads the value the tenant’s way, day first. Only form reports from those tenants land here. The parser fix does not touch this: the swap happens before the parser sees the date. The old table already marked these “not this ticket”, and they had no ticket of their own until now. The desk will not fix it: the booking-v3 form is retired.

one-day-move: dates move by one day, not by a month

These reports describe a day lost or gained, not a swap: Tuesday shown as Monday, a meeting held on two days for clients in Lisbon and Toronto, an invite sent before the booking existed, every June booking one day off. A day/month swap cannot produce any of them. That pattern points at time zones: a date-only value turned into a moment and shown in another offset. The desk had noted these reports (desk-notes.json) and the old table counted them under parser with a footnote.

How the counts are made

  • One report, one count: the export repeats some rows; uniqueById keeps each id once.
  • parser and hint come from causes.ts, by tenant locale, booking form and channel, as before.
  • one-day-move is the desk’s own list in desk-notes.json, taken out of parser.
  • Intake’s tag (meta.intakeTag) is not used: it is guessed from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant in both groups.

Code: src/lib/kb/problems.ts and ProblemTable.svelte. Nothing on this page is typed that the data can count.
